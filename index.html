Litany of Sacrifice

All praise to the Emperor, for our lives belong to Him.
From the ashes of Krieg we rise, bound by blood and sacred duty.

We ask not for victory, only for the chance to fall in His name.
Our blood is the ink with which we write our repentance.
Our lives are the currency we pay for ancient sins.

In life, war.
In death, peace.
In shame, service.
In contrition, atonement.
